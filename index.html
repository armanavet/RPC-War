How to play

  • Every piece moves one square in any direction — like a chess king.
  • Move onto an enemy piece to fight it:
beats rock crushes scissors
beats scissors cut paper
beats paper covers rock
  • Same type vs same type = both pieces die.
  • Losing attacker dies, defender stays put.
  • Win by landing any piece on your opponent's back row — or by taking every enemy piece.

Move markers: ✓ you win the fight · = trade · ✕ you lose the piece.
