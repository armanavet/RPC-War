Tideline
  • Ground you take stays taken. At the end of your turn, every square you dominate that touches ground you already own becomes yours — permanently, until somebody takes it back. The border between the colours is the tideline, and moving it is the whole game.
  • Your army cannot outrun its territory. A unit may only finish its move on your ground or on a square touching it. There are no raids into the enemy rear; there is a line, and it moves at the speed ground can be converted.
  • Attacking is a bet. Step onto ground they own and you must take it that same turn. A unit still standing on their ground when your next turn begins is thrown back to your own line.
  • Domination, not presence. A square only changes hands where you out-project them by four or more. Marginal ground does not move.
  • Build points arrive every turn — two, plus one for each town you own — and buy new units anywhere on your own ground. A wide front is worth having even where it is quiet.
  • Win by taking two thirds of the board, or by killing the enemy general.
