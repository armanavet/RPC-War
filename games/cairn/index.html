Cairn
  • A stack moves exactly as many squares as it is tall, in a straight line, and jumps over anything on the way. Only where it lands matters.
  • Land on any stack and yours goes on top. The top piece owns the whole pile — including every enemy piece buried underneath.
  • A stack taller than three spills: the bottom pieces fall out and are gone for good.
  • You lose when you are down to six pieces, or control no stacks at all.
