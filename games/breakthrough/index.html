Breakthrough
  • Control does not move the line. Pressure does. Each turn, every square of their line you are leaning on hard enough gains a point of pressure. You can see theirs building, and they can see yours.
  • Three connected squares at breaking point and the sector cracks. The units holding it are routed backwards, a hole opens, and the pressure is spent making it.
  • A crack buys you three free moves — but only armour and recon may make them, and inside a breakthrough nothing stops them. Infantry cannot exploit a hole they just made. That is what armour is for.
  • An intact line is impassable. Outside a breakthrough every unit is held up by enemy zones of control. There is no way round; there is only through.
  • Win by taking both supply depots in their half and holding them through a reply. A goal you have to hold cannot be raced for.
  • A headquarters displaces rather than dies. Overwhelm it and it falls back; it is only lost when there is nowhere left to go.
